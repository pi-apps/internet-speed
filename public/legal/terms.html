2. What the service is

The site offers three things:

  • An embedded speed test provided by OpenSpeedTest, which runs on their servers and measures download and upload throughput.
  • A latency test that measures round-trip time from your browser to a list of well-known public services.
  • Optional storage of your own latency results, and aggregate anonymous statistics built from everyone's results.

The service is provided free of charge and is funded by advertising and voluntary donations. No account is required for any measurement feature; an account is needed only to save results across devices, to enter the leaderboard, or to donate.

3. Accuracy and limitations

Every figure on this site is an estimate produced under conditions we do not control. In particular:

  • Results depend on your device, browser, Wi-Fi, router, local network, provider, and the load on the servers being measured.
  • The latency test measures a full HTTPS round trip, including DNS, TCP and TLS. It reads higher than a command-line ping and is not a substitute for it.
  • Speed test results are produced entirely by OpenSpeedTest. We do not see, verify, or store them.
  • Aggregate statistics reflect only the people who happened to test here, and are not a representative survey of any provider or country.

Do not rely on these numbers where accuracy matters legally or commercially — for a contractual dispute with a provider, for example, use the measurement method that provider or your regulator recognises.
